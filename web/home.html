    What we check

    The pre-purchase checklist, automated

    Every item an experienced spec builder asks about before buying a lot. Each comes back clear, watch, flag or verify, with its source.

    01

    Land & zoning

    Parcel boundary, acreage, assessor value, jurisdiction, zoning district and its setbacks.

    UGRC parcels · county GIS · land use code
    02

    Water rights

    Culinary water provider, irrigation companies and places of use, nearby wells and water rights.

    Utah Division of Water Rights
    03

    Geologic hazards

    Mapped landslides, active faults, liquefaction, shallow groundwater, flood zones, seismic category.

    Utah Geological Survey · FEMA · USGS
    04

    Septic & perc

    Sewer availability, soil suitability for absorption fields, water table depth, source protection zones.

    USDA soil survey · health districts
    05

    Power, gas & internet

    Utility providers and evidence of service nearby.

    UGRC utilities · county parcels
    06

    Access & fire

    Road frontage, wildland-urban interface, nearest fire station and hydrant.

    UGRC roads · county GIS · Utah FFSL
    07

    CC&Rs & design review

    Subdivision and PUD status, recorded covenants, architectural review to expect.

    County recorder
    08

    Survey & topo

    Slope, relief and drainage direction from 1-meter LiDAR, before you order a survey.

    USGS 3DEP LiDAR
    09

    Permits & code

    Permitting authority, adopted codes, design loads and fees.

    Jurisdiction · state code
    10

    How it works

    From address to dossier

    Public maps don't replace a survey, a geotech report or a perc test. They tell you which of those you'll need, roughly what they'll cost, and where your plan will have to change, before you spend money on the lot.

    1

    Locate the parcel

    We match the address or parcel ID to the county record and its exact boundary.

    2

    Check every layer

    The boundary is tested against state, county and federal maps. If you add a plan, it's read and compared with what we find.

    3

    Get the dossier

    Red flags, plan conflicts, cost adders and next steps. Print it or save it as a file to share.

    Flag: likely material cost or risk Watch: adds cost or steps Verify: needs a document or site test Clear: nothing found
    Lot Dossier Screening tool built on public data. Not a survey, geotechnical report, title report or appraisal.
