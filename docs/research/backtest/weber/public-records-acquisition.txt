1. Weber County: 2550 South roadway project comparable-sales exhibits

Please provide the publicly releasable comparable-sales exhibits, sale grids and
supporting property/listing sheets from completed land appraisals and administrative
compensation estimates for the 2550 South Roadway Improvement Project, prepared
from 2021 through 2025. Electronic copies of existing records are preferred.

We seek historical market transactions used as comparables, including their sale
date, sale amount, parcel/address, area, property type, improvements at sale and
service/access descriptions. We do not seek owners' bank records, signatures,
personal financial statements or other unrelated personal information. Redacted
copies of the relevant exhibits are acceptable. A listing or index of available
reports would also be useful. Please advise before incurring any charge.

Public locator: https://www.utah.gov/pmn/files/1123245.pdf identifies 35+ appraisal
reviews for this Weber County project. The public commission document index is
https://www.webercountyutah.gov/Transparency/commission_meetings.php .
