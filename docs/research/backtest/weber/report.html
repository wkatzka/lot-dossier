Method

Train on the first five chronological Weber sales and hold out the final two together. Fixed-penalty ridge regression learns log-price relationships using nonlinear acreage, submarket, utility categories and market date. Unknown attributes stay unknown. No target price, listing price, close remarks or later sale enters prediction features. No tuning on the held-out prices.

Median-price baseline MAE: $170,000; acreage-rate baseline MAE: $63,738. The separate expanding-window diagnostic scores 0 of 4 within $50k, MAE $199,316. It overlaps the primary test; it is not independent replication.

Evidence and limitations

  • Only seven research transactions; this is a feasibility experiment, not validated market accuracy.
  • Training prices are from earlier sales, but source packets were published later. No historical publicly-available-as-of replay established.
  • County-hosted MLS sold reports support recorded amounts; closing statements and arm-length status not independently verified.
  • Valley tracts and western county lots differ; size, geography and date are confounded in this sample.
  • No interval calibration set; no calibrated prediction interval provided.
  • No current 2026 accuracy or ±$50k guarantee can be inferred.
  • Hyperparameter is a fixed engineering choice, not empirically validated on independent Weber data.

Off-market and reported below-market sales are retained with flags. An exclusion sensitivity, every training ID, support warning, coefficient, baseline and prediction are preserved in results.json. Original inputs and exclusions are in sales.json.
