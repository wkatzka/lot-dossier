### What the historical architectural terms actually establish

[Master declaration 1882728](https://wolfcreekresortmasterhoa.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/1882728-18-Oct-2002.pdf#page=10), §§4.1–4.3 (PDF pp. 9–11), requires written review before covered work starts. Section 4.2 transfers jurisdiction to the association's DRB after termination of declarant rights. The recorded 2013 termination therefore supplies a meaningful historical governance link. Neither establishes current legal coverage of Cobabe Lot 4. The 2023 master minutes excluding Cobabe, the 2025 proposal preserving master covenants, and any later neighborhood declaration/delegation must be reconciled. These clauses alone do not establish approval before municipal submission.

## Access dependency: demonstrated vs not demonstrated

Demonstrated: the [recorder service](https://www.webercountyutah.gov/Recorder_Surveyor/abstract_title_reg.php) offers subscription access; the [copy-order form](https://www.webercountyutah.gov/forms/document/) requires a document number and payment; Frontier requires sign-in.

Not demonstrated: that either disputed number is the needed agreement; that Frontier case 21920 contains an executed copy; that paying or logging in would resolve the covenant/committee chain; or that no free copy exists elsewhere. These gaps are **attempted but unresolved**, not proven externally blocked.
