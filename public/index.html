    Coverage

    What we check

    Property conditions that affect buildability, cost and permitting. Document coverage is deepest in the Ogden Valley pilot; availability varies by jurisdiction.

    01

    Land & zoning

    Mapped parcel boundary, acreage, assessor value, jurisdiction, zoning and setback standards.

    UGRC parcels · county GIS · land use code
    02

    Water rights

    Culinary water provider, irrigation companies and places of use, nearby wells and water rights.

    Utah Division of Water Rights
    03

    Geologic hazards

    Mapped landslides, active faults, liquefaction, shallow groundwater, flood zones, seismic category.

    Utah Geological Survey · FEMA · USGS
    04

    Septic & perc

    Sewer availability, soil suitability for absorption fields, water table depth, source protection zones.

    USDA soil survey · health districts
    05

    Power, gas & internet

    Utility providers and evidence of service nearby.

    UGRC utilities · county parcels
    06

    Access & fire

    Road frontage, wildland-urban interface, nearest fire station and hydrant.

    UGRC roads · county GIS · Utah FFSL
    07

    CC&Rs & design review

    Subdivision status, covenant references, HOA registration and historic districts. Full covenant text and design approval may remain outstanding.

    County recorder · Utah HOA registry
    08

    Survey & topo

    Slope, relief and drainage direction from elevation data. Mapped boundaries and terrain are not a survey.

    USGS 3DEP LiDAR
    09

    Permits & code

    Who approves your plans and how to reach them, the city office, adopted codes, design loads and fees.

    Jurisdiction · state code
    10

    Plan vs. lot

    Extracted or entered dimensions, foundation and design loads compared with available site findings. Placement and approvals remain separate checks.

    Your plan, read in the browser
    11

    Home performance & restrictions

    Energy code, wood-burning bans, impervious-surface and stormwater rules, water-wise landscaping, checked against your plan.

    Utah code · DEQ · local ordinances
    12

    Easements & recorded restrictions

    All linked plat sheets, potential utility, access, drainage and unusual easement references, with explicit gaps where title records or geometry remain unverified.

    Recorded plats · county parcel references
    13

    Pool feasibility

    Pilot-city pool rules alongside groundwater, terrain, easements and utility constraints. Pool placement and engineering are not established.

    City pool code · site evidence · state pool code
    Report excerpt · published rule

    A finding, its source and what remains open.

    Ogden Valley CityPrivate in-ground poolsRule checked November 7, 2026
    Published requirement

    Pool and machinery: at least 10 feet from property lines.

    The city allows permanent in-ground pools to the side or rear as an accessory use. A larger setback required by the zone takes precedence.

    Source: Ogden Valley City Land Use Code, §108-7-18, pages 16–17
    Still to establish for a property

    Applicable zone setbacks, recorded restrictions and the proposed pool location. This published rule does not establish that a pool fits a particular lot.

    An actual rule used in Cornerpin reports. This excerpt is not a completed property review.

    Sources & verification

    Public records. Mapped conditions. Open questions.

    Source references

    Findings identify their source: Utah UGRC parcels, county GIS and recorder records, UGS hazard maps, USDA soil surveys, USGS elevation, FEMA flood mapping and local codes. Dates are included where supplied; a report date is not a source-update date.

    Evidence limits

    A mapped soil condition is not a geotechnical result. A parcel polygon is not a survey. Failed lookups and missing records remain open; they do not establish an absence of restrictions.

    Cost basis

    Site conditions identify items to price. Current planning ranges are unvalidated placeholders, not lot-specific estimates or contractor bids. Published fees are cited separately where available.

    Permit preparation

    The site report carries property information into a draft application packet.

    01

    Application details

    Parcel details, zoning, design loads and plan information. Estimates are identified for review.

    02

    Supporting requests

    Lot-specific letters for utility service, fire review and HOA requirements where contacts are available. Review them and send from your email.

    03

    Submission checklist

    Documents gathered, drafts prepared and items still missing. Signatures, professional reports and approvals remain identifiable requirements.

    Cornerpin prepares the packet. You review the details and submit through the permitting authority; automatic filing is not available yet.
